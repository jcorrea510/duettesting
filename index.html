How this was made

Nothing here was modelled, painted or photographed.

Every mountain, cloud, wave and star is computed from math, for every pixel, every frame, on your own device. There are no 3D models, no textures and no images. Nothing is downloaded but the code.

Ground
Continents and mountain ranges are a single noise function wrapped around a sphere. Each pixel sends out a ray and marches it forward until it meets that surface.
Sky
Sunlight is scattered through a simulated atmosphere (Rayleigh and Mie scattering). That one model gives the blue day, the red dusk and the glowing rim you see from orbit.
Clouds and sea
Clouds are volumes that rays step through, lit by the sun and casting shadows on the land below. The ocean's waves, reflections and shallows are shaded per pixel too.
One shot
The camera falls from orbit to a few metres above the sand without a cut or a loading screen, because there is nothing to load.
